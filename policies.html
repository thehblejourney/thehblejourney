01

Fulfillment & Delivery

Standard packages purchased through HBLE's website are governed by the package description shown at checkout and these policies. Custom projects should have a written scope describing the requested services, deliverables, price, revision allowance and target schedule before production begins.

Project timelines begin after required materials, onboarding information, approvals and any required payment or deposit have been received. Digital deliverables are normally provided electronically through a download link, review platform, cloud delivery service or other method stated in the project agreement.

Turnaround varies by scope. Any estimated delivery date may change if the scope changes, client materials arrive late, approvals are delayed, or circumstances outside HBLE's reasonable control occur. HBLE will communicate material delays and work with the client on a revised delivery plan. Rush delivery is available only when expressly accepted in writing and may include an additional fee.

02

Refund & Dispute Policy

Because creative services involve reserved production time and work performed specifically for each client, refunds are evaluated based on the work completed and costs already committed.

  • If HBLE has not begun work, amounts paid for unperformed services may be refundable, less any clearly disclosed non-refundable third-party costs or reservation fees.
  • After work begins, any refund will take into account completed work, approved milestones, licensed assets, outside services and other committed project costs.
  • Completed or delivered creative work is generally non-refundable except where required by law or where HBLE materially fails to provide the contracted service.
  • Custom project terms in a signed proposal, invoice, checkout description or contract control if they differ from this general policy.

If you believe a charge or delivery is incorrect, contact HBLE through the support form on the website before filing a payment dispute so we can review the project record and work toward a resolution.

03

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Clients may request cancellation or rescheduling in writing. Fees already earned for work performed remain due. Non-refundable third-party charges and specifically disclosed reservation costs may also remain due. Any remaining unearned balance will be handled according to the applicable project agreement and refund policy.

A package deposit is credited toward the total package price and is not treated as an additional fee. If a client cancels, refund treatment for the deposit follows the same rules above based on work performed, production time reserved and costs already committed unless different written terms were agreed for that project.

HBLE may pause or terminate work for non-payment, prolonged lack of client response, unlawful requests, abusive conduct, material scope changes without agreement, or other material breach. HBLE will provide reasonable notice when practical.

04

Privacy Policy

HBLE Enterprises Inc. collects information that clients voluntarily provide, such as name, email address, company or artist name, project details, files, feedback and billing-related information necessary to provide services.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, perform contracted services, communicate about projects, process or reconcile payments, prevent fraud, maintain business records and comply with legal obligations.

HBLE may use service providers for website hosting, form processing, cloud storage, project review, analytics and payment processing. Those providers receive information only as needed to perform their services. HBLE does not sell customer personal information.

Payment-card details entered into a third-party payment processor's secure checkout are handled by that processor. HBLE does not intentionally store full payment-card numbers on this website.

Business records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for project delivery, accounting, legal compliance, dispute prevention and legitimate business purposes. To request access, correction or deletion of information where applicable, use the support form on the HBLE website.

05

Payments & Card Security

Prices shown on the HBLE website are in U.S. dollars (USD) unless otherwise stated. Standard package pricing is shown on the applicable checkout page. Custom project pricing is confirmed in the client's proposal, invoice or agreement.

HBLE may offer either full payment or a 50% package deposit. A deposit is credited toward the total package price. The remaining balance, if any, is due according to the project onboarding terms, proposal or invoice and must be paid before final delivery unless HBLE agrees otherwise in writing.

Online card checkout is provided by Stripe or another secure payment processor. Payment-card information is transmitted through the processor's checkout environment rather than being directly stored by HBLE on this website.

Clients are responsible for providing accurate billing and project information and for reviewing the package description, project scope and policy terms before payment.
