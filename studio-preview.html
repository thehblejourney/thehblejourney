The HBLE Process

Discover. Build. Refine. Finish.

Discover

We identify the audience, emotional target, references, scope, deliverables, and what the project actually needs to accomplish.

Build

The edit, pacing, color language, motion, sound, and visual direction come together as one system.

Refine

Feedback is handled through a structured revision process so changes improve the work instead of diluting it.

Finish

Approved masters are polished and delivered for the platforms, campaigns, and audiences they were built for.
