The HBLE Process

Discover. Build. Refine. Deliver.

Premium work needs a professional client experience. You know what is being created, what is included and how the project moves forward before the edit gets deep.

01 / DISCOVER

Creative Brief

We align on story, audience, references, deliverables, timeline, budget and what the audience should feel.

02 / BUILD

Production

HBLE develops the cut, visual language, motion, color direction, sound and supporting creative elements.

03 / REFINE

Review

You review the work through an organized revision process tied to the approved scope and included revision rounds.

04 / DELIVER

Final Masters

Approved digital masters are prepared in the agreed formats and delivered electronically after applicable payment terms are satisfied.

Why HBLE
