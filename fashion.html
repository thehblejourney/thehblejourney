How to measure

Use a soft tape over thin clothing. Keep it level and snug, not tight. Ask someone to help with shoulder and sleeve measurements.

Bust

Around the fullest part of the chest.

Waist

Around the natural waist, keeping the tape level.

Hips

Around the fullest part of the hips and seat.

Shoulder width

Across the back from shoulder point to shoulder point.

Sleeve length

From shoulder point to wrist with arm slightly bent.

Inseam

From crotch to desired trouser hem.

Jacket length

From high shoulder point to desired jacket hem.
