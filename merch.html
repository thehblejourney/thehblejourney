Custom Fit & Sizing Guide

Luxury should fit you. Use this guide to prepare your measurements for a personalized HBLE tracksuit. Final sizing and customization are confirmed before an order is accepted.

Your custom fit profile
MeasurementWhat to measure
Chest / BustFullest part of the chest, tape level
WaistNatural waistline, without pulling tight
HipsFullest part of hips and seat
InseamInner leg, from crotch to desired hem
SleeveShoulder point to preferred cuff length
HeightFull standing height, without shoes

Record all measurements in inches or centimeters and use the same unit throughout.

Classic Fit

A comfortable, easy-wearing silhouette. Discuss your preferred room through the body and leg when ordering.

Tailored Fit

A more shaped silhouette based on your individual measurements and preferred finish.

This is a measurement guide, not a finalized garment size chart. Exact size ranges, tolerances, fabric stretch, and custom-order availability must be confirmed with the clothing designer before purchase.
